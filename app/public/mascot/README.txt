VINTAGE COMPUTER MASCOT REFERENCES

Contents:
- 20 individually cropped source illustrations as PNG files
- 13 rigged retro device bodies and saved generation prompts under rig/

The source illustrations are not animation frames. Each live mascot keeps one
body, with motion applied to its SVG face, screen and built-in controls. See
ANIMATION_MAP.md and /mascot-preview.html for the task and hover behaviors.

Canva editable design:
https://www.canva.com/d/XBDeCVYMLKXdktH

Note: The PNGs are separate image assets, so each tile can be moved, resized, or replaced. The Canva Magic Layers conversion is intended to expose independently editable elements in the Canva design. As with any automated image-to-layer conversion, inspect the layers and refine small details if needed; complex shading and overlapping shapes may not separate perfectly.
